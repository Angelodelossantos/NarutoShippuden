Itachi Uchina

Itachi Uchiha is one of the most powerful and complex characters in Naruto Shippuden. A prodigy of the Uchiha clan, he mastered advanced ninja techniques at a very young age and awakened the Sharingan early in life. His Sharingan later evolved into the Mangekyō Sharingan, granting him devastating abilities such as Tsukuyomi, a powerful genjutsu that traps opponents in an illusion, and Amaterasu, black flames that burn anything in their path. He also possessed Susanoo, a massive spiritual warrior that protected him and wielded legendary weapons like the Totsuka Blade and Yata Mirror.
