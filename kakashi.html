Kakashi

Kakashi Hatake, known as the “Copy Ninja,” is one of the most skilled and intelligent shinobi in the Naruto series. As a former member of the elite Anbu Black Ops and the leader of Team 7, he trained Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura. Kakashi gained fame for his Sharingan, which he received from his teammate Obito Uchiha. With it, he could copy over a thousand jutsu, making him extremely versatile in battle. His signature technique, the Lightning Blade (Chidori), is a powerful high-speed attack capable of piercing almost any defense.
