Sakura

Sakura Haruno is a highly skilled kunoichi from the Hidden Leaf Village and a member of Team 7 alongside Naruto and Sasuke. Initially less physically powerful than her teammates, she grows into one of the strongest medical ninjas in the series under Tsunade’s mentorship. Sakura possesses superhuman strength, precise chakra control, and advanced healing abilities, allowing her to both protect and fight on the front lines.
