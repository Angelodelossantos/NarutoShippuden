Rock lee

Rock Lee is a ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who is famous for his incredible dedication and hard work, compensating for his inability to use ninjutsu or genjutsu. Instead, he specializes in Taijutsu, or hand-to-hand combat, and trains his body to peak physical condition, giving him extraordinary speed, strength, and agility. Lee can open several of the Eight Gates, inner chakra limits, which drastically boost his power at the cost of risking his life and body.
