Orochimaru

Orochimaru is one of the most dangerous and cunning villains in the Naruto series. A former member of the Legendary Sannin, he is obsessed with immortality and learning all jutsu in the ninja world. To achieve this, he experiments with forbidden techniques, including body transfers that allow him to switch bodies and extend his life indefinitely. He is also a master of snake-based ninjutsu, capable of summoning giant snakes, stretching his body, and using deadly attacks.
