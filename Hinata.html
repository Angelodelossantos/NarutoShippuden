Hinata

Hinata Hyuga is a skilled kunoichi from the Hyuga Clan, known for her mastery of the Byakugan. The Byakugan grants her nearly 360-degree vision and the ability to see chakra pathways within an opponent’s body. This allows her to use the Gentle Fist fighting style, which targets chakra points to disrupt an enemy’s energy flow and cause internal damage.
