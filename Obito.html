Obito

Obito Uchiha is one of the most powerful and complex characters in the Naruto series. Originally a kind-hearted ninja, he was believed dead after being crushed in a mission, but he was saved and manipulated by Madara Uchiha. Obito inherited the Sharingan from his friend Kakashi and later awakened the Mangekyō Sharingan, gaining the ability to use Kamui, a space–time technique that lets him teleport parts of his body or even entire people and attacks to another dimension.
