Jiraiya

Jiraiya is one of the Legendary Sannin and one of the most powerful ninja in the Naruto series. Known as the “Toad Sage,” he trained under the Third Hokage and later became the mentor of both Minato Namikaze and Naruto Uzumaki. Jiraiya specialized in powerful ninjutsu, including massive Rasengan attacks, Fire Release techniques, and summoning giant toads like Gamabunta. His Sage Mode greatly enhanced his strength, speed, and sensory abilities by drawing on natural energy.
