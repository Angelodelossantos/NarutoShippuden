kawaki

Kawaki is a central character in Boruto: Naruto Next Generations and one of the most powerful young shinobi in the series. He was raised in harsh conditions and later taken in by the Kara organization, where he was given the Kāma mark by Isshiki Ōtsutsuki. The Kāma grants Kawaki enhanced strength, speed, and durability, along with the ability to absorb ninjutsu and open space-time portals. His body was scientifically modified, allowing him to transform his limbs into powerful weapons during battle.
