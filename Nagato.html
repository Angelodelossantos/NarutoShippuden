Nagato

Nagato, also known as Pain, is one of the most powerful and feared shinobi in the Naruto series. He was a former student of Jiraiya and later became the leader of the Akatsuki. Nagato wields the Rinnegan, a legendary dojutsu that grants him god-like abilities, including mastery over all six paths techniques: controlling life and death, summoning giant creatures, absorbing chakra, creating powerful gravitational attacks, and even reviving the dead.
