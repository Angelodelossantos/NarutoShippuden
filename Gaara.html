Gaara

Gaara is one of the strongest shinobi in the Naruto series, known for his powerful control over sand. As the former jinchūriki of Shukaku, the One-Tailed Beast, he once had immense destructive power and nearly unstoppable defense. His sand automatically protects him from attacks, forming a shield without him needing to think. He can also manipulate large amounts of sand to crush opponents, create massive waves, or seal enemies with techniques like Sand Coffin and Sand Burial.
