Sasuke Uchiha

Sasuke Uchiha is one of the most powerful and skilled ninjas in the Naruto series, and a key rival and ally to Naruto Uzumaki. Born into the Uchiha clan, he awakened the Sharingan at a young age, giving him extraordinary perception and the ability to copy jutsu. Later, he evolves it into the Mangekyō Sharingan and eventually the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan, granting him devastating techniques like Amaterasu, Susanoo, and space–time manipulation.
