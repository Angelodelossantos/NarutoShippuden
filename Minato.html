Minato

Minato Namikaze, known as the “Yellow Flash of the Leaf,” is one of the most legendary shinobi in the Naruto series. Renowned for his incredible speed, he developed the Flying Thunder God Technique, which allows him to teleport instantly to marked locations, making him nearly untouchable in battle. Minato was also a master of space-time ninjutsu and created the Rasengan, a powerful spinning chakra sphere that became one of Naruto’s signature techniques.
