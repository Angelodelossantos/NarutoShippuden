Naruto

Naruto Uzumaki is the main protagonist of Naruto and Naruto Shippuden, and later becomes a central figure in Boruto. Born as the host of Kurama, the Nine-Tails Fox, he possesses immense chakra and stamina, allowing him to perform powerful techniques like the Shadow Clone Jutsu and Rasengan. Over time, Naruto masters Sage Mode, enhancing his strength, speed, and sensory abilities, and later learns to cooperate with Kurama to access the Nine-Tails Chakra Mode, giving him near-godlike power on the battlefield.
